What happened

Counter-UAS led the month with 254 stories, down 5. Contracts & industry (230) and Ukraine / front line (126) followed. Attention shifted towards Saildrone (15, up from 3), L3Harris (11, up from 5) and Performance Drone Works (7, up from 1), while D-Fend Solutions (7) and Airwise Solutions (6) arrive after no coverage at all the month before. Going the other way, Anduril (46 stories down to 20) and BAE Systems (28 stories down to 3). The most widely reported story was “Patria to join with Ukraine’s General Cherry to make drones in Finland”, carried by 7 outlets. Across the month there were 782 stories in total, down 9% on the month before.
