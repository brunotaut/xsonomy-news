October 2026

Monthly digest · 1635 defence stories, distilled to the 20 that mattered

What happened

Counter-UAS led the month with 522 stories, up 202 on the previous month. Contracts & industry (497) and Ukraine / front line (232) followed, with Contracts & industry and Ukraine / front line gaining ground. Attention shifted towards BAE Systems (54, up from 9), DJI (118, up from 79) and Boeing (53, up from 20), while ANRA Technologies (14) and SIG Sauer (12) arrive after no coverage at all the month before. Going the other way, DroneShield (27 stories down to 13) and Amprius Technologies (10 stories down to 2). The most widely reported story was “L3Harris delivers first VAMPIRE counter-drone systems to US Army”, carried by 25 outlets. Across the month there were 1635 stories in total, up 44% on the month before.
