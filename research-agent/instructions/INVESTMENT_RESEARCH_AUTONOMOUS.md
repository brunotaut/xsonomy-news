## Each execution

1. Read latest repository head and state. If a different `active_run` has a nonexpired lease, do no work and return no update. Acquire a two-hour lease through a commit with expected-head compare-and-swap before any DB writes. On a rejected branch lease read the new head and preserve concurrent changes. Do not force push. Clear the lease after saving the result; an expired lease may be reclaimed. Persist all findings before stopping.
2. Continue a meaningful internal group, normally 20–40 companies if source quality permits. Prioritise unreviewed public companies with strong financing candidates, then other public companies, then partial histories, then the rest of the cohort. Do not spend the run only rebuilding queues or reporting status.
3. Verify the exact legal or economic issuer, aliases and group relationships. A subsidiary or similarly named Canadian venture's funding is not automatically the parent's. A change of brand requires evidence of continuity. Avoid duplicate investor identities; retain source names/vehicles and explain successor mappings.
4. Research public history through the actual checked date. Use at least (a) a broad dated financing/history search, (b) company news and official financial/registry disclosures, and (c) investor/transaction-party searches including older rounds and new strategic investments. Verify source pages, not just aggregator snippets. Search across appropriate local languages when useful. Existing candidate groups are a starting point, not a completed review.
5. Extract only supported events, amounts in original currency, announced dates at established precision, stage, financing instrument, closing status, exact versus approximate/lower/upper-bound values, participants and explicitly supported lead roles. Leave missing data null. Link sources per event and per investor. Never infer individual cheques, unnamed investors, valuation as funding, primary proceeds from secondary purchases, or company grant proceeds from an entire consortium budget. Government grants, debt and equity must remain distinct. Do not sum currencies, cumulative totals and extensions into total_funding. Do not turn a disclosed aggregate of historical financing into a new single round.
6. Reconcile every candidate claim: import confirmed facts, explain unsupported/conflicting claims in a source-level ledger, and record scoped negative findings only after genuine research. An empty DB, an aggregator's missing entry or a failed fetch is not evidence of no funding. Unknown private details do not prevent completing bounded public-source research, but must remain visibly unknown.
7. Use versioned JSON manifests matching `scripts/prepare-funding-pilot.mjs`. Inspect current schema and importer before changes. Validate and generate SQL, run meaningful PostgreSQL tests and a transaction dry run against production with rollback. Verify baseline after rollback, then apply atomically and verify counts, actual facts, source constraints and idempotency. Existing conflicting facts require an explicit reviewed correction with expected-old-value checks and a reversal record, not silent overwrite or a second artificial event. Preserve existing curated investor profiles and legacy relationships. No raw article bodies in the repository.
8. Update queue, FUNDING_STATE and dated reports honestly; counters come from actual DB queries. Commit with a lease against the latest head. Use `checked_at` for work actually performed, never for queued identities. Save source-backed holds and continue other records without asking Nazar to solve public-source uncertainty.
9. When new approved DB facts are ready, append a short source-commit/validation entry to the current catalogue `docs/STATUS.md` and commit to its main branch to trigger the existing push build. Verify successful deployment and live `/data/landscape.json` plus `/data/landscape-investors.json`. Do not claim publication from DB success alone. The catalogue repository remains read-only to Supabase. Avoid needless frontend changes or duplicate build triggers.

## Review status and completion

Queue state and database coverage have different meanings. `reviewed_public_sources` means the bounded public-source research above has been completed; DB coverage may remain `partial` if history or known claims remain unresolved. `no_public_round_found` is a scoped research result, never a claim that a company raised zero money. Document searches, actual pages checked and limitations. A duplicate/non-company/out-of-scope disposition needs identity evidence, not an automatic inference from name, country or publication status.

Complete only when every cohort identity has an evidenced disposition, all supported imports/corrections have been reconciled and live publication checked, and remaining unresolvable source limitations are explicitly reported. Run a final coverage/source/duplicate/amount audit and reconcile new database identities. Mark state complete; send one concise final report to Nazar linking the site and audit report. Pause the continuation automation using its ID stored in FUNDING_STATE. Do not invent an ETA or claim exhaustive knowledge of undisclosed private financings.

Real blockers include missing connector access or permissions, a destructive decision outside scope, or a required additional paid service/budget. First preserve a checkpoint and work around the issue where possible. An individual inaccessible source is a research hold, not a user blocker. If blocked, explain the exact action needed once and avoid repeated notifications for the same blocker.
